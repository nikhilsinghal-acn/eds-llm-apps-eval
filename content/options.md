## Compatibility

Not every combination can be built. The following restrictions apply across all Terra trims.

| Option | Restriction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 22-inch gloss black wheels | Terra Dynamic SE only | The 22-inch wheel and tyre package requires the uprated brake hardware fitted only to Dynamic SE |
| Black Exterior Pack | Not available with Arctic White | The pack's gloss black trim is not colour-matched for Arctic White |
| Panoramic roof | Not available with Cold Climate Pack | The panoramic glass assembly cannot accommodate the pack's additional roof insulation |
| 22-inch gloss black wheels | Not available with Cold Climate Pack | The low-profile tyre is not rated for the pack's cold-weather specification |

If you select an incompatible combination in the configurator, the restricted option is removed and the reason is shown.
