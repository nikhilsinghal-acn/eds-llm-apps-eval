# Veyra Terra

A mid-size SUV with five seats, a 2.0-litre 250 hp petrol engine and all-wheel drive as standard. Available in two trims and configurable across paint, wheels, packs and roof.

## Trims

| Trim | Code | Engine | Drive | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Terra S AWD | s-awd | 2.0 petrol, 250 hp | All-wheel drive | ₹26,40,000 |
| Terra Dynamic SE | dynamic-se | 2.0 petrol, 250 hp | All-wheel drive | ₹31,80,000 |

## Standard on every Terra

Adaptive cruise control, lane keep assist, dual-zone climate control, 12.3-inch touchscreen, wireless phone charging, powered tailgate, LED headlamps, six airbags, hill descent control.

## Additional on Dynamic SE

Uprated front brake discs, perforated leather upholstery, blind spot monitoring, 360-degree camera, 14-speaker audio, adaptive dampers.
