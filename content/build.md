# How to Build Your Terra

Configuring a Terra takes four steps. Each step validates against the previous one, so you only ever see combinations that can actually be built.

## Step 1 — Choose a trim

Terra S AWD or Terra Dynamic SE. Your trim determines which wheels are available later, so this is the first decision.

## Step 2 — Choose a paint finish

Four finishes. Arctic White is included; Slate Grey and Obsidian Black add ₹95,000; Crimson Red adds ₹1,45,000. Your paint choice affects pack availability.

## Step 3 — Choose wheels

20-inch gloss silver is included. The 21-inch satin dark is available on both trims. The 22-inch gloss black requires Dynamic SE.

## Step 4 — Choose packs and roof

The Black Exterior Pack and the Cold Climate Pack can each be added, and a panoramic roof is available. Some combinations are restricted — see [compatibility](/options).

## Pricing and next steps

Your configurator shows a running ex-showroom price for Delhi. Registration, insurance and applicable taxes are added at the dealer.

When you finish, your build is saved with a reference number you can take to any [Veyra dealer](/dealers), or continue on the Veyra app.
